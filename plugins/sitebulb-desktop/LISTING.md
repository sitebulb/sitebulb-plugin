## Name

Sitebulb Desktop

## One-liner

Read-only access to the technical SEO audits in Sitebulb Desktop on your Windows or Mac computer.

## Description

Connect Claude to Sitebulb Desktop to explore the technical SEO audits stored on your Windows or Mac computer. Ask about your projects and audits, audit summaries and reports, hints and insights, sample affected URLs, the data for a single URL, history across audits, and what changed between two audits.

Access is read-only, so the connector can't start crawls, change settings or delete data. Sitebulb Desktop needs to be open and running on your computer, and you sign in to your Sitebulb account with OAuth. You need a Sitebulb Desktop licence and at least one finished audit.

The Sitebulb Desktop plugin adds five technical SEO skills that use this connector: what to fix first, what changed, technical SEO audit, dev handoff and release check. For Sitebulb Cloud audits, use the separate Sitebulb Cloud connector.
